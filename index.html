What is ISN?

Institut Sukan Negara (ISN) is Malaysia's National Sports Institute. We use sports science, sports medicine and technology to help Malaysian athletes train smarter, recover faster and win on the world stage.

Who we areRead Act 729
ISN HQ · Kompleks Sukan Negara, Bukit Jalil
01

Sports Science

Physiology, nutrition, psychology, biomechanics and performance analysis that make athletes faster, stronger and sharper.

02

Sports Medicine

The National Sports Medicine Centre (PPSN): specialist clinic, physiotherapy, radiology, medical lab and pharmacy.

03

Technology & Research

Research, innovation and athlete data that keep Malaysian sport ahead of its rivals.
