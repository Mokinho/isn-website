About the service

What is medical laboratory

The Sports Medicine Division runs laboratory analysis, clinical lab testing and sample management for athletes and patients. Results feed directly into medical care, nutrition plans and training decisions.

Common uses include health screening, monitoring iron and vitamin status, checking recovery and illness markers through heavy training, and supporting diagnosis at the specialist clinic.

Inside the service
