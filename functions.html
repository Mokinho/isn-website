Our role,
by law

Institut Sukan Negara was established under the National Sports Institute Act 2011 (Act 729) on 16 September 2011. The Act sets out ten functions that guide everything ISN does for Malaysian sport.

Akta 729

The ten functions

Under section 4 of Act 729, the functions of the Institute are to:

  1. 01Advise the Minister on any matter relating to the scientific aspects of athletes’ performance.
  2. 02Formulate, recommend and review scientific methods and measures that improve athletes’ performance.
  3. 03Work with government entities, public and private universities, sports bodies and other organisations on programmes that identify athletes with the potential to excel in sport.
  4. 04Provide scientific, advisory, consultancy and related services in sports science and sports medicine.
  5. 05Build and keep relationships and cooperation with any person, body or organisation involved in developing sports science and sports medicine, in and outside Malaysia.
  6. 06Provide and carry out the medical examination and treatment that athletes need.
  7. 07Act as the information, resource and reference centre for sports science and sports medicine.
  8. 08Collect, manage and share information, and keep records, on all matters relating to the scientific aspects of athletes’ performance.
  9. 09Carry out and coordinate research, development and innovation in sports science, sports medicine and other related fields.
  10. 10Do anything else it considers appropriate to carry out its functions effectively, or that is incidental to them.
