Cara memohon · How to apply

Apply in 3 steps

  • Download the application form and the full requirements for the position from the ISN vacancies page.
  • Email the completed application to kerjaya@isn.gov.my, with the position name and grade in the subject line.
  • Only shortlisted candidates are called for interview. If you have not heard back within three months of the closing date, your application was not successful.
  • Incomplete or late applications, or applications that do not meet the requirements, will be rejected.
Latihan industri · Industrial training

Industrial training

Internships for students from local and overseas universities.

  • Malaysian citizens currently studying at a public or private university in Malaysia or abroad.
  • A CGPA of 3.4 and above (applications below 2.8 are considered case by case).
  • Apply to any division or centre that fits your course, subject to places.
  • Apply at least 30 days before your internship starts. Internships are unpaid and you must have insurance from your university.
  • Email the application form, your university’s letter and your résumé to praktikal@isn.gov.my.
